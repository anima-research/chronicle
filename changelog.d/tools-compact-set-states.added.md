- `tools/compact-set-states.py` reclaims `records.log` space held by superseded whole-state writes: for the named states it drops every record before the newest terminal operation (`Set`, `Snapshot`, `Delta`), re-links the surviving chains, remaps `state.bin`, and leaves the originals as `*.pre-compact`. Dry-run by default; refuses an open store, a multi-branch store, a stale `state.bin`, or a torn log. On a production store whose kv-unified presentation receipt (context-manager #148) was 92% of a 2.15 GB log: 201 MB afterwards, every state's reconstructed value byte-identical.
- `tools/state-digests.mjs` prints a digest of every state's reconstructed value plus record and blob counts, so a store can be compared before and after compaction (or against a copy) with `diff`.
